Product · AI Assistant

Ask your data in plain English

A governed assistant that answers business questions from your own data. It runs only a fixed set of approved, read-only queries, so it can explain patterns but can never expose or dump records.

How it stays safe

Helpful, but governed by design

Approved questions only

A fixed toolset of pre-written queries. The assistant picks one; it never writes its own SQL.

Read-only

The connection cannot insert, update or delete. Writes are physically impossible.

No bulk export

It returns patterns and summaries, and refuses requests to dump full records.

Plain-language answers

Replies in clear business English, with the numbers behind them.

Your data only

Runs on the same prepared data behind your report and dashboard.

Runs anywhere

Lives in the browser over your committed database, no server required.
