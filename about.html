About Pau Analytics

Data Should Make Life Easier.

Here's how we work with it.

Pau Analytics believes data should make life easier, not more complicated. We help Malaysian SME owners use what they already have in a way that feels natural, practical, and genuinely useful — so decisions aren't guesses, but informed steps forward.

Data can feel overwhelming, but it doesn't have to be. The way we work is simple: take what you already have — your till, your inventory system, your WhatsApp order log — uncover the patterns that matter, and turn them into clear answers you can act on.

The focus is on solving real problems. Which three products are quietly losing money. Why weekend sales outrun the weekday plan. Which customers are about to leave before anyone notices. Every analysis is framed from the owner's point of view, not from a textbook — whether the question is about inventory, marketing, customer retention, or pricing.

What we build depends on the question. Think of your business like a car. Our analyst report is a full inspection — a clear picture of what's running well, what needs attention, and what's about to break before the owner makes the next big decision. Our dashboard is the driver's own panel — live readings of speed, fuel, and engine health, so changes are caught at a glance without re-reading the whole report. And our AI agent is a smart driving assistant — it keeps watching after the workshop, catches minor issues early before they become costly, and guides the business to run more smoothly every day.

Most SME owners we work with have been running their businesses for years. Nobody knows the shop, the stock, the customers better than the people who show up every day. We don't pretend otherwise. What we offer is a second angle — the patterns in the numbers that an outside eye can sometimes catch faster. If that sounds useful, we're here.
