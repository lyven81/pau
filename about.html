About

Data should make life easier, not harder

Pau Analytics helps Malaysian SME owners use the data they already have in a way that feels natural, practical and genuinely useful, so decisions get easier, not more complicated.

The way we work is simple: take what you already have, your till, your inventory system, your WhatsApp order log, prepare it, and uncover the patterns that matter. Which products quietly lose money. Why weekends outrun the weekday plan. Which customers are about to leave before anyone notices.

What we build depends on the question. The analyst report is a full inspection of what is running well and what needs attention; the dashboard is the live gauge you check at a glance; the AI assistant answers as you go. We also handle the messy first step, preparing and structuring your data so good analysis no longer waits on messy spreadsheets.

Most owners we work with have run their business for years and know the shop, the stock and the customers better than anyone. We don't pretend otherwise. Our job is to make that knowledge legible in the data, and put a clear next step in front of it.
